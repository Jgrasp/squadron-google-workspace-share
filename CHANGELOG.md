# Changelog

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-16

- The extension has its logo, shown on the Share with the team entry too.

## 0.2.1 · 2026-11-13

- Stop sharing no longer fails when Google refuses to delete the Apps Script project: the link is cut and the project stays in your Drive.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-13

- A subagent on Sonnet does the share in the artifact's conversation, with its progress on the Artifacts panel.
- Shared artifacts are cleaned up after 90 days by default, which you can change in the extension's settings.
- The shared link is the one of your domain, which opens even when the browser's default Google account is another one.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-12

- First version: shares an html artifact with your Workspace domain as a Google Apps Script web app, with a state everyone edits together.
